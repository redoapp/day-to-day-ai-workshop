# Draft a status update

Turn messy input (notes, links, half-sentences) into an update someone busy can act on.

## Steps

1. **Gather.** Ask for the raw material if it wasn't given: what shipped, what's in
   progress, what's blocked, and any numbers worth citing.
2. **Check the wiki.** Read [`wiki/about-me.md`](../../wiki/about-me.md) and
   [`wiki/taste.md`](../../wiki/taste.md) so the voice and altitude match the person.
3. **Group by theme**, not by chronology. Lead with outcomes, not activity.
4. **Write it** in this shape:
   - **TL;DR** — one sentence.
   - **Shipped** — 2–4 bullets, each with the *result*, not the effort.
   - **In progress** — what's moving and the next step.
   - **Blocked / needs a decision** — be explicit about what you need from whom.
5. **Tighten.** Cut anything that isn't skimmable in 30 seconds.

## Done when

- A manager could read it in under a minute and know exactly what to do.
- Every bullet states an outcome or a clear ask — no vague "worked on X."
- The voice matches the wiki, not generic corporate filler.
