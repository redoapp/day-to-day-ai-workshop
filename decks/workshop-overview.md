# Your AI Workbench

From chatbot to coworker — context, tools, a clear finish line.

*(Present with the Slides plugin: `Cmd/Ctrl+P → "Slides: Start presentation"`.)*

---

## The shift

- Most AI use = a search engine with **amnesia**
- A knowledge base **compounds** — it gets smarter every time you touch it
- You collect; the AI compiles, answers, and files it back

---

## What you'll leave with

- A personal LLM **knowledge base** (`raw/` → `wiki/`)
- A reusable way to **teach AI** how you work (`CLAUDE.md`, `_operator.md`)
- A **deliverable** built from it — this deck, or a site

---

## The loop

1. Drop sources in `raw/`
2. `/wiki-ingest` → the AI compiles a wiki
3. `/wiki-query` → cited answers, filed back
4. `/wiki-lint` → health check

---

## Now you try

Run `/wiki-start` and point it at a topic you actually care about.
