# Taste

> Your standards. This is what lets AI match *you* instead of producing generic output.

## Writing
*Voice, length, formality. Do you hate em-dashes? Love bullet points? Say it plainly.*

## Decisions
*How do you weigh speed vs. polish? When is "good enough" actually good enough?*

## Things I consistently dislike
*The patterns that make you go "no, redo that." Be specific — examples beat adjectives.*

## Things I consistently like
*Reference work you admire. "Make it more like X" is a powerful instruction.*
