# Workflows

> Recurring things you do. The more precisely you describe one, the easier it is to turn
> it into a skill later (see [`/skills`](../skills/)).

## A workflow worth capturing
*Name it. What triggers it? What are the steps? What does "done" look like?*

Example shape:

### Weekly status update
- **Trigger:** Friday, end of day.
- **Inputs:** my notes, what shipped, what's blocked.
- **Steps:** gather → group by theme → write 5 bullets → flag risks.
- **Done when:** it's skimmable in 30 seconds and a manager could act on it.
