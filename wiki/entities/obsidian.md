# Obsidian

A free, local-first markdown app. It treats a folder of `.md` files as a **vault**, renders
`[[wikilinks]]` as a navigable **Graph View**, and is extensible via plugins like [[dataview]].
In an [[llm-knowledge-base]] it's the *reading* surface — the AI writes the files, you browse
them here. [[hoeem-2026-llm-knowledge-base-course]] [[karpathy-2026-llm-knowledge-bases]]
