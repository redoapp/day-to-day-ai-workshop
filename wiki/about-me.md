# About me

> Fill this in with your AI. Delete the prompts in italics as you answer them.

## Role
*What's your job? Who do you work with? What are you responsible for?*

## What I care about
*What outcomes matter to you? What do you optimize for? What do you refuse to compromise on?*

## What good work looks like
*When AI hands you something, how do you judge it? What makes you trust it vs. redo it?*

## What I am NOT
*What should AI avoid assuming about me? (e.g. "I'm not an engineer — explain accordingly.")*

## Context that's easy to miss
*Acronyms, team names, internal tools, the way your org talks. The stuff a new hire wouldn't know.*
