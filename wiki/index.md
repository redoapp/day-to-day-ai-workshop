## Overview

a mature wiki: interlinked concepts, entities, a synthesis, and a filed query answer. See the
live [[dashboard]]. Point [`CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md)'s Overview at your own topic, clear these
entries (`/wiki-start`), and ingest your own sources.
